Privacy

Your tower stays on your device.

This policy explains how Block Tower handles data. It applies to the iOS app, Android app, and this website.

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Block Tower does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking services. We handle data only as needed to provide game features.

Data we handle

Game data on your device

High scores, game progress, Bolt, settings, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device. Block Tower does not collect this data on a server.

Information you send us

Our support form is hosted by Google Forms. Your submitted answers are shared with Block Tower so we can understand and handle your request. If you include contact details, we use them to reply.

In-app purchases

On iOS, Bolt and other in-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store and StoreKit. Apple manages payment details directly; Block Tower does not receive your full payment information. Apple may use purchase records to verify transactions and provide purchase features. The Android version has no in-app purchases.

System APIs

The iOS app uses Apple UserDefaults, and the Android app uses libGDX local preferences, to store progress and settings. The iOS game uses system uptime for timers and revive protection; Android uses a monotonic clock to manage haptic feedback intervals. These functions process data on your device and do not send it to us.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear progress in the app settings, delete the app, or otherwise clear the device data. Block Tower keeps support form responses only as long as needed to handle the request, then deletes them as reasonably required by operational and legal needs.

Children and third-party services

Block Tower does not collect children's data for personalized advertising or tracking. Google Forms hosts the support form; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles related data. Apart from Apple services needed for the iOS App Store and in-app purchases, we currently do not integrate other third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Policy changes

If our data practices change significantly, we will update this page and its effective date. Please check back for the latest version.
